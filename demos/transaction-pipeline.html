Transaction Data-Quality Pipeline

The real pipeline validates 11,000+ invoice and payment lines for a national restaurant-chain client. This page runs the same four checks in your browser on a synthetic data set, so you can see exactly what the pipeline catches and how the exceptions reach a reviewer.

All data on this page is synthetic. Maple Fork Restaurant Group, its locations, products, prices and every number below were generated for this demo and are recalculated live in your browser. No real business data is shown.
